Match Five

Match Five is a word puzzle. You get five words and five headings at a time, and many of the words fit more than one heading. Fill every slot with a word that fits, and five more words and headings arrive, often wanting the slots you just used. There are eight levels.

How to play

  • Tap a word, then tap the heading it belongs under. On a computer you can also drag it.
  • A slot turns green when its word fits its heading and red when it does not.
  • "Rose" fits "Red", but it also fits "Flower". When a later word fits only one of those slots, Rose has to move.
  • When every slot so far is green, five more words and five more headings arrive. Fill them all to finish the level.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of puzzle is Match Five?

A word-sorting puzzle. You put each word under a heading it belongs to. Many words fit more than one heading, and the next five words often want the slots you already filled, so you keep rethinking earlier choices.

Is Match Five free?

Yes. It plays in your browser with no download, no account, and no payment.

How do I win a level?

Fill every slot with a word that belongs under its heading. A slot turns green when its word fits and red when it does not. When every slot so far is green, five more words and five more headings arrive. Fill those too and the level is done. There are eight levels.
